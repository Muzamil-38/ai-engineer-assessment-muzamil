## biography | Identity and birthplace
Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in Funchal, Portugal, on 5 February 1985.
He played as a forward. CR7 refers to Cristiano Ronaldo.

## career | Real Madrid years
His Real Madrid spell ran from 2009 to 2018. His presentation at the Bernabeu took place
on 6 July 2009.

## goals | Appearances and goals
Real Madrid credits Ronaldo with 451 goals across 438 competitive matches.
The club lists 312 league goals and 105 Champions League goals for Madrid.

## honours | Trophies with Real Madrid
His club honours include four Champions Leagues, three Club World Cups, three UEFA Super
Cups, two league titles, two Copas del Rey and two Spanish Super Cups.

## records | Scoring records
The profile identifies him as Madrid's record goalscorer. It highlights a 61-goal club
season and a 17-goal Champions League campaign.

## clubs | Club career beyond Real Madrid
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo
Wikipedia's profile describes Ronaldo's youth development at Andorinha, Nacional and
Sporting CP. His senior Sporting spell preceded Manchester United (2003-2009), Real Madrid
(2009-2018) and Juventus (2018-2021). He returned to United in 2021 and left in 2022.
He joined Al-Nassr in 2023. The article reviewed on 24 September 2026 lists Al-Nassr as
his club and number 7 as his shirt number; this is a dated snapshot.

## portugal | Portugal national team
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo
Ronaldo first represented Portugal's senior side in 2003, aged 18. His first international
goal came at Euro 2004. He captained Portugal to the Euro 2016 title and won the Nations
League in 2019 and 2025. These are national-team achievements, not club honours.

## individual_awards | Career-wide individual awards
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo
Wikipedia lists five Ballon d'Or awards and four European Golden Shoes across his career.
His first Ballon d'Or was awarded in 2008, when he was 23. The other wins were in 2013,
2014, 2016 and 2017. These totals are broader than his Real Madrid spell.
